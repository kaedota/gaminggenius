CUSTOMER SUPPORT / GAMING GENIUS STATION

Refund & cancellation policy

Updated 28 November 2026. This policy covers purchases and gaming bookings made through gaminggenius.my.

Need help with a payment, booking or product? Contact GG with your order or booking number. Paid refunds are reviewed by staff and are not issued automatically by the website.

1. Gaming bookings

  • Browsing, selecting a time and requesting a member TAC do not reserve a station. When a five-minute hold is shown for an ordinary FPX game booking, entering FPX checkout temporarily holds one available physical station for five minutes. Successful payment confirms that same booking. The three lobby PS5 stations can support three independent overlapping checkouts when all three are available; a further checkout cannot start when the pool is full. Historical checkout requests without a displayed hold remain unreserved. Member-balance bookings allocate a station only after TAC-authorised payment succeeds.
  • When the five-minute countdown ends, the website checks the original bank bill; it does not assume payment failed or release the station from the countdown alone. A failed or cancelled checkout releases its hold only after the server confirms the original bill was unpaid and closed. Unknown bank results remain protected for review. If payment is received after the hold was closed or the booked service cannot be provided, the payment is retained for staff review and is not automatically refunded or treated as a confirmed booking. Contact GG to arrange an agreed alternative or a refund for the unavailable service.
  • Request cancellation or a change before your session starts through your account, where available, or contact GG. Staff review paid cancellations, changes and any refund due; submitting a cancellation request does not automatically refund payment.
  • If GG cannot provide your confirmed session, contact us to arrange a replacement session or refund for the unavailable service.
  • For late arrival, a missed session or a request after the session starts, contact GG for a review. Do not assume the session time or payment will automatically be extended, credited or refunded.
  • GTA Play Pass: RM350 buys 30 two-hour sessions by FPX; member balance and points cannot purchase the pass. Website vouchers are valid for 90 days from successful payment, with at most one redemption per member per Malaysian service day. A fresh member-phone TAC authorises a redemption. Cancel at least 2 hours before the start to return the original voucher with its original expiry; later cancellation or no-show consumes the voucher. This voucher return is not a cash refund.

2. Merchandise, returns & pre-orders

  • Check your items, quantities, jersey size and delivery details before payment. For a cancellation or change, contact us as soon as possible. We check whether fulfilment or pre-order production has already started before confirming available options.
  • Jersey orders are pre-orders, with an estimated lead time of 2–3 weeks. This is an estimate. Mixed orders are delivered or collected together when all items are ready. Contact GG if your order is delayed or you need to discuss cancellation.
  • If an item arrives damaged, faulty, incorrect or incomplete, contact us promptly with your order number, a description and available photos of the item and packaging. Keep the item and packaging while we review a suitable replacement, return or refund. Contact us before sending anything back so we can provide instructions and confirm any shipping arrangements.
  • For a change of mind or an incorrectly selected size, contact GG before using the item. Available return or exchange options depend on the order status and item condition and will be confirmed by staff.
  • If we cannot fulfil a paid item, we will contact you to arrange an alternative acceptable to you or a refund for the unavailable item.

3. Payment and refund processing

FPX payments are processed by Billplz. If your bank shows a deduction but GG still shows pending payment, contact GG and do not pay again until we check the transaction. Report suspected duplicate payments with both transaction references.

We confirm the approved refund amount, method and expected processing time after reviewing your request. Refunds are returned through the original payment method where supported; otherwise we will agree a suitable method with you. Bank and payment-provider processing times may vary.

GG Member balance payments, points redemption and top-ups, where available, require staff review for cancellation, reversal or refund. Cancelling a booking does not automatically return member balance or points. This policy does not remove any applicable consumer rights.

如付款、预约或商品出现问题，请提供订单或预约编号联系 GG。已付款交易的退款由员工审核，网站不会自动退款。

  • 浏览、选择时段及申请会员TAC均不占机位。普通游戏FPX预约如显示5分钟暂留，进入FPX结账时会暂留一台有空位的实际机位5分钟，付款成功后确认原机位预约。大厅3台PS5均有空位时可同时进行3笔独立的重叠时段结账；名额用尽后不能再开始另一笔付款。未显示暂留的历史请求仍不占位。会员余额预约仍在TAC授权并成功扣款后才分配机位。
  • 5分钟倒数结束后，网站会先核对原银行账单，不会单凭倒数结束就认定付款失败或释放机位。失败或取消的结账，须由服务器确认原账单未付款并已关闭后才释放；银行结果不明确时继续保护原记录并交由核对。如暂留关闭后才收到款项，或无法提供已付款时段，将保留收款并由员工处理，不会自动退款，也不视为已确认预约。请联系GG安排双方同意的替代服务，或退还无法提供部分的款项。
  • 如需取消或更改，请在游玩开始前通过账号内提供的功能操作，或联系 GG。已付款预约的取消、更改及退款须经员工审核；提交取消申请不代表已自动退款。
  • 如 GG 无法提供已确认的游玩服务，请联系我们安排替代时段，或退还无法提供部分的款项。
  • 迟到、未到场或开始后才提出更改，请联系 GG 审核。游玩时间、款项或余额不会因此自动延长、补回或退还。
  • GTA游玩配套：RM350通过FPX购买30张每次2小时的兑换券，不能使用会员余额或积分购买。官网游玩券从付款成功起90天有效，每位会员每天最多兑换一次，兑换时使用会员手机TAC验证。开场前至少2小时取消，退回原券并保留原到期日；不足2小时取消或未到场将扣券。退券不等于退回现金。
