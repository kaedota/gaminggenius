Which events count?

Only events explicitly announced as GG season points events before registration. Each eligible event uses the same scale. No automatic inclusion of past events or other organisers' events. One verified final standing per GG player ID per event; no-show and disqualified entries are excluded before import.

Why the best six results?

Your six highest-scoring eligible results contribute to your total. Extra appearances offer another chance to improve, without making attendance alone the deciding factor. One placement award per event; participation points are not added on top.

How are ties and corrections handled?

Equal points are ordered by best counted placing, then the number of wins among counted results. If still tied, players share the same rank. Event standings must be verified before entry; tied tournament placings receive the same placement points. Contact GG to request a correction with the event name and result evidence. Season dates and correction deadlines will be published before launch.
