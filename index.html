Which events count?

Only events announced as GG season points events before registration. Each eligible event uses the same scale. Past events and other organisers’ tournaments are not automatically included. Each GG player ID receives one verified placement award per event; no-shows and disqualified entries are excluded.

Why the best six results?

Your six highest-scoring eligible results contribute to your total. Extra appearances give you another chance to improve. Participation points are not added on top of placement points.

Ties, results and corrections

Ties use best counted placing, then wins among counted results. Remaining ties share the same rank. Tied tournament placings receive the same points. Contact GG with the event and result evidence to request a correction. The correction deadline will be published before launch.
