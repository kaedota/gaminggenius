Which events count?

Only events explicitly designated by GG as eligible Beyblade ranking events count. Halloween Night on 30 October 2026 is the first Season 01 ranking event. Regular events use ×1; GG-designated double-points events use ×2 across every points band. The multiplier must be announced before registration. Event size alone does not grant double points. Past events and other organisers’ tournaments are not automatically included. Each unique Player ID receives one total award per event: the highest applicable band only. No-shows, registration-only entries and disqualified players earn zero. Top Cut must be confirmed, not inferred from attendance. Multi-slot/session scoring must be agreed before results are entered; separate slot/session awards are not assumed.

How do season totals work?

Every verified event result adds to your season total, without a best-N limit. For example, a regular champion earns 20 total, not 20 + 7 + 5. Corrections replace the original player/event record; saving again does not add points twice. Seasons are independent: final standings and point histories remain available, and a new season starts from zero.

Ties, results and corrections

Ties use best placing, then wins within the season. Remaining ties share the same rank. Tied tournament placings receive the same points. Contact GG with the event and result evidence to request a correction. Follow the correction deadline announced for the event.
