PRIVACY

Updated 31 October 2026.

Gaming Genius uses Google Analytics to understand website visits, page views and referring websites. Reports are restricted to authorised administrators; visitor counts and dashboard links are not displayed on this website.

Analytics uses a first-party browser cookie to estimate distinct visitors and visits. This identifies a browser, not a verified person. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled in our website integration.

We do not send the text typed into the ranking search box, player names, phone numbers, form entries or player-result dialog content to Analytics. Reported pages use predefined section names and approved event IDs. URL query parameters are excluded, and referring URLs are reduced to the referring website origin. Automatic site-search and form measurement are disabled.

We honour the browser Do Not Track signal. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out tool or block Analytics in your browser. This will not affect the event archive or rankings.
